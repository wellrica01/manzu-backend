# MEDICATION DISCOVERY PLATFORM

## Transforming Healthcare Access in Northern Nigeria

---

### THE PROBLEM WE SOLVE

Over 230 million Nigerians face a daily struggle: finding available medications at transparent prices.

Patients spend hours searching pharmacy to pharmacy, often traveling across cities for specialized medications. Pharmacies lose customers daily because people simply can't find them. The current system is broken \- it's inefficient, opaque, and leaves millions underserved nationwide.

*"I spent 3 days searching for my diabetes medication across Kano before finding it at a pharmacy 15km away."* \- Typical patient experience

---

### OUR SOLUTION

We are building Nigeria's first comprehensive medication aggregation platform \- a location-based marketplace that connects patients with nearby pharmacies that have their needed medications in stock, with transparent pricing.

For Patients:

* Search any NAFDAC-approved medication instantly  
* Compare prices across multiple nearby pharmacies  
* See real-time availability and stock levels  
* Order for delivery or pickup with verified prescriptions  
* Access medications previously impossible to find locally

For Pharmacies:

* Reach customers who would never find them otherwise  
* Manage inventory through integrated mobile app  
* Access patients seeking specialized medications  
* Grow revenue through expanded customer base  
* Gain market intelligence and analytics

---

### OUR UNIQUE ADVANTAGE

First-Mover in Aggregation Model: Unlike individual pharmacy e-commerce sites, we aggregate all pharmacies in a location, creating a comprehensive medication discovery engine.

Strategic Market Entry: We're launching in Northern Nigeria \- an underserved market with 50+ million people and minimal digital health infrastructure \- before expanding nationwide.

Complete ATC Classification: Our database covers Level 1-5 ATC medication classifications, making us the most comprehensive medication search platform in Nigeria.

Location-Free Service: Patients can find medications anywhere in their city, not just from their usual pharmacy.
